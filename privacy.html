Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

The short version

Where’s Wren? does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code in the app. Everything you do in the game stays on your iPhone or iPad. The App Store privacy label for the app is “Data Not Collected.”

What stays on your device

The app stores a small amount of information locally so the game works:

  • Player names, colors, and avatar choices you enter on the “Who’s playing?” screen.
  • Your guesses, results, points, streaks, Passport stamps, and badges.
  • Settings such as your preferred globe style and whether the daily reminder is on.

This information never leaves the device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Location

The game is about guessing where Wren is, not where you are. The app never asks for or uses your location.

The globe

The globe is drawn with Apple Maps (MapKit). When you spin it, your device requests map imagery from Apple in the same way the built-in Maps app does. That request is governed by Apple’s privacy policy, and it does not include your guesses or anything about you from the game.

Daily puzzles

Puzzles are bundled with the app. In a future version the app may download new puzzles from our content server; those downloads will contain nothing about you beyond the ordinary technical details of any web request, and we will update this page before that ships.

Notifications

You can turn on an optional daily reminder. It is scheduled locally on your device and does not use a push server.

Children

Where’s Wren? is rated 4+ and made for families. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app hands your result grid to the iOS share sheet. What happens next is up to you and the app you choose to share with.

Changes

If we ever change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the App Store.
