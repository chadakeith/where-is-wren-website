Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Draft. This policy is a starting template and has not yet been reviewed against the app's actual data practices.

Overview

Where is Wren ("the app") is an iOS application published by Chad Keith. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

The app is designed to collect as little as possible. Depending on the features you use, the app may process:

  • Location data, only if you grant location permission, and only while the app needs it to provide its core feature.
  • Data you enter into the app, which is stored on your device and, if you enable it, in your personal iCloud account.
  • Diagnostic data such as crash reports, which Apple may share with us in aggregated, anonymized form if you have opted in through iOS settings.

How we use information

Information is used solely to provide and improve the app's features. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third parties

The app does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Data synced through iCloud is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Data lives on your device and in your iCloud account, under your control. Deleting the app removes its local data. You can remove iCloud data through iOS Settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@whereiswren.com.
